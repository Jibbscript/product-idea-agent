# Demand Signal Scoring Rubric

Detailed methodology for calculating signal strength scores.

## Overall Score Calculation

The final signal score (1-10) is a weighted average of component scores:

| Component | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Search Volume | 30% | Monthly search volume for core keywords |
| Search Growth | 20% | Year-over-year trend direction |
| Community Size | 25% | Active community members across platforms |
| Engagement Quality | 15% | Depth of discussions, pain point intensity |
| Willingness to Pay | 10% | Evidence of spending intent |

## Component Scoring

### Search Volume (30%)

| Score | Monthly Volume | Example Keywords |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| 10 | >100,000 | "project management", "email marketing" |
| 9 | 50,000-100,000 | "time tracking software", "invoice app" |
| 8 | 20,000-50,000 | "OKR software", "employee scheduling" |
| 7 | 10,000-20,000 | "SOC 2 compliance", "sales enablement" |
| 6 | 5,000-10,000 | "api documentation tool" |
| 5 | 2,000-5,000 | Niche B2B keywords |
| 4 | 1,000-2,000 | Very specific solutions |
| 3 | 500-1,000 | Emerging categories |
| 2 | 100-500 | Early-stage markets |
| 1 | <100 | No measurable search demand |

### Search Growth (20%)

| Score | YoY Change | Interpretation |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| 10 | >100% growth | Explosive growth, emerging category |
| 9 | 75-100% growth | Very strong upward trend |
| 8 | 50-75% growth | Strong growth trajectory |
| 7 | 25-50% growth | Healthy growth |
| 6 | 10-25% growth | Moderate growth |
| 5 | 0-10% growth | Stable/slight growth |
| 4 | 0-10% decline | Slight decline |
| 3 | 10-25% decline | Moderate decline |
| 2 | 25-50% decline | Significant decline |
| 1 | >50% decline | Category dying |

### Community Size (25%)

| Score | Total Active Members | Platforms Counted |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|
| 10 | >1,000,000 | Reddit + Forums + Social |
| 9 | 500,000-1,000,000 | Combined across platforms |
| 8 | 200,000-500,000 | Strong multi-platform presence |
| 7 | 100,000-200,000 | One major community |
| 6 | 50,000-100,000 | Medium-sized community |
| 5 | 20,000-50,000 | Established niche |
| 4 | 10,000-20,000 | Small but active |
| 3 | 5,000-10,000 | Early community |
| 2 | 1,000-5,000 | Very small |
| 1 | <1,000 | No community |

### Engagement Quality (15%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| 10 | Daily posts, high reply rates, strong emotion, spending discussions |
| 9 | Multiple daily posts, detailed problem descriptions |
| 8 | Regular posting, active troubleshooting threads |
| 7 | Weekly activity, some problem-focused discussions |
| 6 | Moderate activity, general discussions |
| 5 | Occasional posts, mixed topics |
| 4 | Infrequent posts, low engagement |
| 3 | Rare activity, superficial discussions |
| 2 | Very little activity |
| 1 | Dead or spam-filled |

### Willingness to Pay (10%)

| Score | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| 10 | Multiple "I would pay $X" statements, active purchasing behavior |
| 9 | Clear price anchoring from existing solutions, upgrade discussions |
| 8 | Complaints about cost of alternatives (implies value perception) |
| 7 | Discussions about ROI, asking about pricing |
| 6 | Some mentions of paying for solutions |
| 5 | Neutral - no clear signals either way |
| 4 | Preference for free solutions mentioned |
| 3 | Resistance to paying expressed |
| 2 | Strong free-only sentiment |
| 1 | No commercial intent visible |

## Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Signal Strength | Recommendation |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| 9-10 | Very Strong | High confidence in demand, proceed aggressively |
| 7-8 | Strong | Good demand signals, worth pursuing |
| 5-6 | Moderate | Some demand exists, validate further |
| 3-4 | Weak | Limited signals, high risk |
| 1-2 | Very Weak | Insufficient demand evidence, reconsider |

## Confidence Levels

Also report a confidence level based on data quality:

| Confidence | Criteria |
|------------|----------|
| High | 5+ sources, recent data (<6 months), quantitative metrics |
| Medium | 3-4 sources, somewhat recent data, mix of quantitative/qualitative |
| Low | 1-2 sources, older data, primarily qualitative |

## Example Calculation

For "AI-powered home energy audit":

| Component | Raw Score | Weight | Weighted |
|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Search Volume (2K/mo) | 5 | 0.30 | 1.5 |
| Search Growth (+45% YoY) | 7 | 0.20 | 1.4 |
| Community (r/homeimprovement 4M) | 8 | 0.25 | 2.0 |
| Engagement (active DIY threads) | 7 | 0.15 | 1.05 |
| WTP (complaints about $300 audits) | 7 | 0.10 | 0.7 |
| **Total** | | | **6.65 → 7** |

Final Signal Score: **7/10** (Strong)
Confidence: **Medium** (3 sources, recent data)
