# Example Idea Briefs

Reference examples showing properly structured idea briefs.

## Example 1: B2C Mobile App

```markdown
# Idea Brief: Home Energy Leak Mapper

## One-Line Pitch
Home Energy Leak Mapper helps homeowners reduce energy bills by 20% by using smartphone thermal scanning to identify insulation gaps and air leaks.

## Problem Statement
Homeowners waste $200-500/year on energy bills due to poor insulation and air leaks they can't see. Professional energy audits cost $300-500 and require scheduling weeks in advance. DIY thermal cameras cost $200+ and require expertise to interpret.

## Target Customer
Homeowners aged 35-55 in US suburban areas with:
- Homes built before 2000 (older insulation standards)
- Annual energy bills over $2,000
- DIY mindset (shop at Home Depot, watch home improvement YouTube)
- Concerned about both cost savings and environmental impact

## Solution Hypothesis
Use the existing thermal sensor in newer smartphones (iPhone 15 Pro+) combined with AI interpretation to provide instant, actionable energy audit results. The app guides users room-by-room, identifies issues, explains severity, and connects them with local contractors for fixes.

Key capabilities:
- Smartphone thermal scanning (no additional hardware)
- AI-powered issue detection and severity scoring
- Room-by-room guided audit workflow
- Cost savings calculator
- Contractor matching marketplace

## Key Assumptions
1. Smartphone thermal sensors are accurate enough to detect meaningful heat loss
2. Homeowners will complete a 30-minute DIY audit process
3. Users will pay $30/year for app subscription (vs $0 for free alternative)
4. Contractor referrals will provide sustainable revenue at 10% commission
5. 20% of scanned issues result in contractor bookings

## Success Metrics

### Validation Phase
- 100 beta testers complete full home audit
- 70% report finding "surprising" issues
- 30% request contractor quotes within 7 days

### Launch Phase
- 10,000 downloads in first month
- 15% conversion to paid subscription
- 500 contractor bookings in first quarter
```

## Example 2: B2B SaaS

```markdown
# Idea Brief: FinComply Auto

## One-Line Pitch
FinComply Auto helps seed-stage fintech startups achieve SOC 2 compliance in 30 days instead of 6 months by automating evidence collection and policy generation.

## Problem Statement
Fintech startups need SOC 2 compliance to close enterprise deals, but the process takes 6+ months and $50K+ with traditional consultants. Early-stage teams lack security expertise and can't afford dedicated compliance staff. Every month of delay costs potential enterprise revenue.

## Target Customer
Seed to Series A fintech startups with:
- 5-30 employees
- B2B business model requiring enterprise sales
- No dedicated security/compliance hire
- Active fundraising or enterprise sales pipeline
- CTO or technical co-founder as decision maker

NOT for: Banks, established fintechs with compliance teams, B2C-only companies

## Solution Hypothesis
Integrate with the tools startups already use (AWS, GitHub, Slack, GSuite) to automatically collect 80% of SOC 2 evidence. Generate policies using templates customized to their stack. Provide a dashboard showing readiness and gap analysis. Connect with auditor partners for final certification.

Key capabilities:
- One-click integrations with 20+ common tools
- Automated evidence collection and monitoring
- AI-generated policies from templates
- Real-time compliance dashboard
- Auditor-ready export package

## Key Assumptions
1. 80% of SOC 2 evidence can be collected automatically via APIs
2. CTOs will trust AI-generated policies (with human review)
3. Startups will pay $500-1,000/month (vs $50K one-time for consultants)
4. We can achieve 30-day time-to-compliance for standard cases
5. Auditor partners will accept our evidence format

## Success Metrics

### Validation Phase
- 20 CTO interviews completed
- 5 design partners signed (free tier)
- 2 successful SOC 2 certifications with partner auditors

### Launch Phase
- 50 paying customers in first 6 months
- $25K MRR by month 6
- <45 day average time-to-compliance
```

## Example 3: Niche Community

```markdown
# Idea Brief: SynthCollective

## One-Line Pitch
SynthCollective helps vintage synthesizer collectors buy, sell, and maintain rare instruments through a curated marketplace and expert community.

## Problem Statement
Vintage synthesizer collectors struggle to find authenticated instruments, fair pricing, and repair expertise. eBay has fraud and no specialization. Reverb.com lacks deep community and authentication. Facebook groups are fragmented and transactions are risky. Collectors need a trusted space.

## Target Customer
Synthesizer collectors and musicians who:
- Own 3+ vintage synthesizers (pre-1990 manufacture)
- Spend $2,000+/year on equipment
- Active in online synth communities
- Value authenticity and provenance documentation
- Located in US, UK, Germany, Japan (major collector markets)

## Solution Hypothesis
Create a curated marketplace with authentication services, provenance tracking, and integrated community features. Charge sellers a 10% commission (vs eBay's 13%+) with added value of authentication. Build community through forums, virtual meetups, and repair guides.

Key capabilities:
- Authenticated listings with provenance documentation
- Expert community for repair and modification advice
- Escrow-protected transactions
- Price history and valuation database
- Integration with repair shops for restoration services

## Key Assumptions
1. Collectors will pay 10% commission for authentication and trust
2. We can build authentication expertise (hire expert advisors)
3. Community features drive repeat engagement and transactions
4. Repair shop partnerships provide additional revenue
5. Market is large enough: 50,000+ active vintage synth collectors globally

## Success Metrics

### Validation Phase
- 500 email waitlist signups
- 50 collector interviews completed
- 3 authentication expert advisors recruited
- 10 repair shop partnership LOIs

### Launch Phase
- 1,000 active listings in first 3 months
- $100K GMV in first quarter
- 70% seller satisfaction score

## Status
Draft
```

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

### Too Vague
❌ "A platform to help people be more productive"

✅ "A focus timer app that helps remote knowledge workers reduce context switching by blocking distracting apps during designated deep work hours"

### Solution-First Thinking
❌ Problem: "People need our AI-powered blockchain solution"

✅ Problem: "Small landlords spend 5+ hours/week on tenant communications and can't afford property management services"

### No Testable Assumptions
❌ "Users will love our product"

✅ "Users will complete the onboarding flow in under 5 minutes (we can test with prototype)"

### Unrealistic Metrics
❌ "1 million users in first month"

✅ "1,000 beta signups from targeted subreddit campaigns"
