# Workflow Guide

Detailed guidance for running the validation workflow.

## Workflow Phases

### Phase 1: Discovery (Steps 1-2)

**Goal**: Capture and validate initial demand for the idea.

#### Step 1: Idea Brief Creation

**Purpose**: Structure the raw idea into a testable hypothesis.

**Key Activities**:
- Extract value proposition
- Define target customer hypothesis
- Document key assumptions

**Quality Checks**:
- Is the problem statement clear?
- Is the target customer specific enough?
- Are assumptions testable?

**Common Issues**:
- Idea too vague → Ask clarifying questions
- Solution-focused → Redirect to problem-first thinking
- Multiple ideas → Help prioritize one

#### Step 2: Demand Signals

**Purpose**: Validate that real demand exists.

**Key Activities**:
- Research search trends
- Mine community discussions
- Gather willingness-to-pay signals

**Quality Checks**:
- Are search volumes meaningful?
- Is community engagement active?
- Are there paying customers for similar solutions?

**Decision Point**: If signal strength < 4/10, consider pivoting before continuing.

### Phase 2: Customer & Market (Steps 3-5)

**Goal**: Deeply understand the customer and market opportunity.

#### Step 3: Problem-Segment

**Purpose**: Define who has this problem most acutely.

**Key Activities**:
- Identify customer segments
- Research demographics/firmographics
- Assess problem severity per segment

**Quality Checks**:
- Is ICP specific enough to identify real people?
- Is problem severity backed by evidence?
- Are purchase behaviors documented?

#### Step 4: Competitive Landscape

**Purpose**: Understand the competitive dynamics.

**Key Activities**:
- Map direct and indirect competitors
- Analyze positioning and pricing
- Identify gaps and opportunities

**Quality Checks**:
- Are all major competitors identified?
- Is differentiation opportunity clear?
- Are threat levels realistic?

#### Step 5: Market Sizing

**Purpose**: Quantify the opportunity.

**Key Activities**:
- Research TAM from industry sources
- Calculate SAM based on ICP
- Estimate realistic SOM

**Quality Checks**:
- Is methodology documented?
- Are sources credible and recent?
- Is SOM estimate realistic?

**Decision Point**: If market size < expectations, revisit ICP or consider adjacent markets.

### Phase 3: Strategy (Steps 6-8)

**Goal**: Define how to win in this market.

#### Step 6: Pricing & WTP

**Purpose**: Determine revenue model and price points.

**Key Activities**:
- Analyze competitor pricing
- Research willingness-to-pay
- Design value ladder

**Quality Checks**:
- Are competitor benchmarks comprehensive?
- Is WTP evidence documented?
- Do unit economics work (LTV:CAC > 3)?

#### Step 7: Solution Wedge

**Purpose**: Define what to build and how to differentiate.

**Key Activities**:
- Prioritize features for MVP
- Define technical approach
- Articulate differentiation

**Quality Checks**:
- Is MVP scope tight enough?
- Is differentiation defensible?
- Is technical approach feasible?

#### Step 8: GTM Channels

**Purpose**: Plan how to reach customers.

**Key Activities**:
- Identify viable channels
- Estimate CAC per channel
- Design growth loops

**Quality Checks**:
- Are channels aligned with ICP behavior?
- Are CAC estimates reasonable?
- Is launch plan actionable?

**Decision Point**: If no viable channels identified, revisit ICP or solution positioning.

### Phase 4: Assessment (Steps 9-10)

**Goal**: Evaluate risks and synthesize findings.

#### Step 9: Risk Assessment

**Purpose**: Identify what could go wrong.

**Key Activities**:
- Brainstorm risks across categories
- Score by severity and likelihood
- Develop mitigations

**Quality Checks**:
- Are all categories covered?
- Are critical risks mitigated?
- Are assumptions documented?

#### Step 10: Scorecard Generation

**Purpose**: Synthesize validation into a decision framework.

**Key Activities**:
- Score each dimension
- Calculate composite score
- Generate recommendation

**Quality Checks**:
- Is evidence cited for each score?
- Is composite calculation correct?
- Is recommendation justified?

### Phase 5: Synthesis (Step 11)

**Goal**: Produce actionable deliverable.

#### Step 11: Validation Report

**Purpose**: Create comprehensive documentation.

**Key Activities**:
- Write executive summary
- Structure findings narratively
- Define next steps

**Quality Checks**:
- Is executive summary clear?
- Are recommendations actionable?
- Is the report scannable?

## Workflow Variations

### Fast Track (2-4 hours)
For quick feasibility assessment:
1. Idea Brief (abbreviated)
2. Demand Signals
3. Competitive Landscape
4. Scorecard (simplified)

### Deep Dive (8-12 hours)
For investment-grade validation:
- All 11 steps with extended research
- Multiple ICP interviews incorporated
- Detailed financial modeling
- Comprehensive competitive analysis

### Pivot Assessment
When re-evaluating after initial findings:
1. Review existing artifacts
2. Re-run demand signals
3. Update ICP if needed
4. Re-run competitive analysis
5. Update scorecard

## Handling Blockers

### Insufficient Data
- Document what's missing
- Lower confidence scores
- Recommend primary research

### Conflicting Findings
- Document both perspectives
- Use conservative estimates
- Flag for validation

### Scope Creep
- Refer back to idea brief
- Stay focused on core problem
- Park tangents for later

### Analysis Paralysis
- Set time limits per step
- Focus on decision-useful insights
- Good enough > perfect

## Quality Assurance

### Per-Step Review
After each step, verify:
- [ ] Output follows artifact contract
- [ ] Evidence is cited
- [ ] Findings are actionable

### Cross-Step Consistency
Periodically check:
- [ ] ICP is consistent across artifacts
- [ ] Numbers align (market size, pricing, etc.)
- [ ] Narrative is coherent

### Final Review
Before delivering report:
- [ ] All 11 artifacts exist
- [ ] Scorecard calculations are correct
- [ ] Recommendation is supported by evidence
- [ ] Next steps are specific and actionable

## Collaboration Tips

### When Working with Others
- Share progress tracker
- Assign step owners
- Schedule sync points after each phase

### When Presenting Findings
- Lead with recommendation
- Use scorecard as summary
- Have detailed artifacts as backup

### When Iterating
- Version artifacts
- Document what changed and why
- Update scorecard after changes
