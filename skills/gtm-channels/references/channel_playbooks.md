# Channel Playbooks

Detailed strategies for major go-to-market channels.

## Content Marketing / SEO

### Overview
Build organic traffic through valuable content optimized for search.

### Timeline
- First results: 3-6 months
- Meaningful traffic: 6-12 months
- Scale potential: 12-24 months

### Strategy
1. **Keyword Research**
   - Identify pain point queries
   - Find low-competition opportunities
   - Map to buyer journey stages

2. **Content Types**
   - How-to guides
   - Comparison pages
   - Tool/template libraries
   - Glossary/educational content

3. **Execution**
   - Start with 10-20 pillar pieces
   - Target 1-2 posts per week
   - Build internal linking structure
   - Focus on quality over quantity

### Metrics
- Organic traffic
- Keyword rankings
- Time on page
- Conversion rate

### Budget
- Low ongoing cost (writer: $500-2K/article)
- SEO tools: $100-500/month
- Time investment is primary cost

## Paid Search (Google Ads)

### Overview
Capture intent-driven traffic through paid search ads.

### Timeline
- First results: 1-2 weeks
- Optimization: 1-3 months
- Predictable: 3-6 months

### Strategy
1. **Keyword Selection**
   - Start with high-intent keywords
   - Exact match for control
   - Negative keywords to exclude waste

2. **Ad Copy**
   - Address pain point directly
   - Include social proof
   - Clear CTA

3. **Landing Pages**
   - Message match with ads
   - Single conversion goal
   - A/B test continuously

### Metrics
- CPC (Cost Per Click)
- CTR (Click-Through Rate)
- Conversion Rate
- CAC

### Budget
- Test phase: $500-2K/month
- Growth phase: $5K-20K/month
- Enterprise: $50K+/month

### Benchmarks
| Industry | Avg CPC | Avg CVR |
|----------|---------|---------|
| B2B SaaS | $3-10 | 2-5% |
| E-commerce | $1-3 | 3-5% |
| Professional Services | $5-20 | 2-4% |

## LinkedIn (B2B)

### Overview
Reach B2B buyers through organic content and personal branding.

### Timeline
- Initial engagement: 2-4 weeks
- Meaningful following: 3-6 months
- Inbound leads: 6-12 months

### Strategy
1. **Personal Branding**
   - Founder posts 3-5x/week
   - Share insights, not promotion
   - Engage with ICP content

2. **Content Types**
   - Industry hot takes
   - Behind-the-scenes building
   - Customer success stories
   - Educational carousels

3. **Engagement**
   - Comment on ICP posts
   - DM warm connections
   - Join relevant groups

### Metrics
- Profile views
- Follower growth
- Post impressions
- Inbound DMs

### Budget
- Free for organic
- Premium: $100/month (Sales Navigator)
- Ads: $1K-10K/month (higher CPM than other platforms)

## Product-Led Growth (Freemium)

### Overview
Acquire users through product usage, convert to paid.

### Timeline
- First users: 1-4 weeks
- Conversion optimization: 2-6 months
- Viral loops: 6-12 months

### Strategy
1. **Free Tier Design**
   - Enough value to be useful
   - Clear upgrade triggers
   - No credit card required for signup

2. **Conversion Triggers**
   - Usage limits
   - Premium features
   - Team/collaboration needs

3. **Viral Mechanics**
   - Invite teammates
   - Share outputs
   - Embed/watermark in free tier

### Metrics
- Signups
- Activation rate
- Free-to-paid conversion
- Viral coefficient (k-factor)

### Benchmarks
- Free to paid: 2-5% (typical)
- Free to paid: 10-25% (exceptional)
- Activation: 20-40% (Day 1)

## Outbound Sales (B2B)

### Overview
Proactively reach decision-makers through direct outreach.

### Timeline
- First meetings: 2-4 weeks
- First deals: 1-3 months
- Predictable pipeline: 6-12 months

### Strategy
1. **Targeting**
   - Build ideal account list
   - Identify decision-makers
   - Research before outreach

2. **Sequences**
   - Email + LinkedIn combination
   - 5-8 touches over 2-3 weeks
   - Personalization at scale

3. **Qualification**
   - BANT or MEDDIC framework
   - Clear ICP fit criteria
   - Disqualify quickly

### Metrics
- Open rate
- Reply rate
- Meeting rate
- Pipeline generated

### Budget
- SDR: $60-100K/year fully loaded
- Tools: $200-500/month per rep
- Expected: 3-5 meetings/week per SDR

### Benchmarks
| Metric | Below Avg | Average | Good |
|--------|-----------|---------|------|
| Open Rate | <20% | 30-40% | >50% |
| Reply Rate | <2% | 3-5% | >8% |
| Meeting Rate | <0.5% | 1-2% | >3% |

## Community Building

### Overview
Build a engaged community around your problem space.

### Timeline
- Initial community: 1-3 months
- Active engagement: 3-6 months
- Self-sustaining: 12+ months

### Strategy
1. **Platform Selection**
   - Slack: Real-time, B2B
   - Discord: Power users, developers
   - Circle: Courses, paid communities
   - Facebook Groups: B2C, older demo

2. **Content Calendar**
   - Daily prompts/questions
   - Weekly live sessions
   - Monthly special events

3. **Moderation**
   - Clear community guidelines
   - Active moderation
   - Recognize top contributors

### Metrics
- Member count
- Weekly active users
- Messages per user
- Event attendance

### Budget
- Mostly time investment
- Platform costs: $0-500/month
- Events/swag: $500-2K/month

## Partnerships

### Overview
Leverage partners for distribution and credibility.

### Timeline
- First partners: 1-3 months
- Meaningful volume: 6-12 months
- Major partnerships: 12-24 months

### Types
1. **Technology Integrations**
   - Native integrations with popular tools
   - Marketplace listings
   - Co-marketing

2. **Agency/Reseller Partners**
   - White-label or referral
   - Training and certification
   - Revenue share

3. **Strategic Partners**
   - Co-development
   - Co-selling
   - Bundling

### Metrics
- Partner-sourced leads
- Partner-influenced revenue
- Partner activation rate

### Budget
- Rev share: 10-30%
- Partner manager: $80-120K/year
- Partner marketing: $10-50K/year
