# Test Idea: PlantBox AI

Subscription box for houseplant enthusiasts with AI-powered care recommendations. Monthly delivery of curated plants matched to your home conditions (light, humidity, experience level). App provides personalized care reminders and troubleshooting.

## Additional Context

- Target: Urban millennials/Gen Z interested in houseplants
- Problem: Plants die due to improper care, intimidating for beginners
- Current solutions: Local nurseries, Amazon plants (no guidance), generic subscription boxes
- Revenue model: Monthly subscription ($35-75/month) + plant shop
- Competition: Bloomscape, The Sill, Horti, generic subscription boxes
