# Market Size Analysis

## Executive Summary
The home energy management market represents a substantial opportunity with strong growth driven by rising energy costs, climate awareness, and smart home adoption. The DIY thermal imaging niche within this market is underserved, with a realistic SAM of about $236M (estimate) for app-based solutions built on clip-on phone thermal cameras, narrowed by how many homeowners can own, buy or borrow one.

## TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- **Size**: $4.5B (US Home Energy Management)
- **Methodology**: Top-down from industry reports
- **Source**: Grand View Research 2024, MarketsandMarkets
- **Growth Rate**: 15.2% CAGR (2024-2030)

The broader home energy management market includes smart thermostats, monitoring systems, audit services, and efficiency products.

## SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
- **Size**: $236M
- **Constraints Applied**:
  - US market only (100% of $4.5B base)
  - DIY segment (35% of homeowners prefer DIY): $1.6B
  - Homes built before 2000 (60% of housing stock): $960M
  - Homeowners who own, borrow or would buy a clip-on thermal camera (25%, an estimate): $236M
- **Calculation**: $4.5B × 0.35 × 0.60 × 0.25 = $236M

## SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
- **Year 1 Target**: $1.2M
- **Assumptions**:
  - 40,000 app downloads
  - 20% free-to-paid conversion at $30/year = $240K subscription revenue
  - 4,800 contractor referrals at $200 average commission = $960K referral revenue
- **Rationale**: Conservative first-year capture of 0.5% of SAM, primarily through subscription and referral revenue streams

## Market Dynamics

**Drivers**:
- Rising energy costs (+20-30% in many regions since 2022)
- Climate awareness and home sustainability interest
- Smart home adoption (60% of US homes have smart devices)
- Affordable clip-on phone thermal cameras (FLIR ONE Gen 3 at $199.99 list, $149.99 on sale in Nov-Dec 2024, with a USB-C version for iPhone 15+ from Dec 2024) and library lending programs that loan them for 1-2 weeks (Arlington VA since 2016, Fairfax County VA since 2017, Montgomery County MD since 2019; no national count exists)

**Constraints**:
- Activation requires owning, buying or borrowing a clip-on thermal camera; only FLIR ONE cameras are supported at launch (Seek Nano has no third-party SDK)
- Seasonal window: insulation scans need at least a 10°C / 18°F indoor-outdoor temperature difference
- Consumer price sensitivity for subscription apps
- Contractor marketplace requires geographic density

**Trends**:
- DIY home improvement boom (accelerated by pandemic, sustained post)
- Utility rebate programs incentivizing efficiency upgrades
- Integration of AI in home management apps

## Confidence Assessment
**Medium** - TAM figures from reputable research firms; SAM calculation uses reasonable assumptions but segments are estimates, including the 25% camera-access share. SOM is conservative but dependent on product-market fit and effective marketing.
