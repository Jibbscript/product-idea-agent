# Go-to-Market Plan

## Channel Strategy

### Primary Channels
| Channel | CAC Est. | Volume | Priority |
|---------|----------|--------|----------|
| Content Marketing/SEO | $8-12 | High | 1 |
| App Store Optimization | $5-10 | Medium | 2 |
| YouTube Tutorials | $10-15 | Medium | 3 |
| Home Improvement Influencers | $15-25 | Medium | 4 |
| Reddit Community | $3-8 | Low-Medium | 5 |

### Channel Details

#### Content Marketing/SEO
- **Why**: ICP actively searches for energy efficiency solutions; high-intent traffic
- **Tactics**:
  - Create pillar content: "Complete Guide to DIY Energy Audits"
  - Target long-tail keywords: "find air leaks in house", "thermal camera for home inspection"
  - Build backlinks through home improvement guest posts
- **Metrics**: Organic traffic, keyword rankings, conversion rate
- **Budget**: $2,000/month for content creation

#### YouTube Tutorials
- **Why**: ICP watches home improvement YouTube; visual medium perfect for thermal imaging
- **Tactics**:
  - Create "How to Use" video series
  - Partner with home improvement YouTubers for reviews
  - Demo videos showing real energy savings
- **Metrics**: Views, subscribers, app downloads from links
- **Budget**: $3,000/month for production + influencer partnerships

## Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch (4 weeks before)
- Build email waitlist via landing page (target: 1,000 signups)
- Seed content on r/homeimprovement, r/HomeOwners
- Reach out to home improvement bloggers for reviews
- Create tutorial video content for launch day

### Launch
- App Store launch with ASO optimization
- Product Hunt submission
- Email waitlist with exclusive early access
- Press outreach to home/tech publications
- Reddit AMA on r/homeimprovement

### Post-Launch (first 90 days)
- Respond to all App Store reviews
- Iterate based on user feedback
- Scale content marketing based on performing keywords
- Begin contractor partnership outreach

## Growth Loops

### Referral Loop
User completes audit → Shares savings results on social → Friends see potential savings → Download app → Repeat

### Content Loop
Publish energy saving content → SEO traffic → App downloads → User success stories → New content ideas → More SEO traffic

## Partnerships

**Utility Companies**: Partner with utilities offering rebate programs; app generates rebate-ready documentation

**Home Improvement Retailers**: Co-marketing opportunities with Home Depot, Lowe's for seasonal energy campaigns

**Contractor Networks**: Revenue share for qualified leads; contractors recommend app to customers
