# Test Idea: Home Energy Leak Mapper

Turn your phone into a thermal camera for DIY home energy audits. Identify air leaks, insulation gaps, and HVAC inefficiencies. Monetize through subscription analytics and contractor referrals.

## Additional Context

- Target: US homeowners with older homes (pre-2000)
- Leverages thermal sensors in newer smartphones (iPhone 15 Pro+)
- Revenue model: Freemium app + contractor referral fees
- Competition: Professional energy auditors ($300-500), expensive thermal cameras ($200+)
