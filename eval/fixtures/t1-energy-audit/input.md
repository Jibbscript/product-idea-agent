# Test Idea: Home Energy Leak Mapper

Pair your phone with an affordable clip-on thermal camera for DIY home energy audits. Identify air leaks, insulation gaps, and HVAC inefficiencies. Monetize through subscription analytics and contractor referrals.

## Additional Context

- Target: US homeowners with older homes (pre-2000)
- Works with clip-on phone thermal cameras (e.g. FLIR ONE, about $150-200) that homeowners own, buy, or borrow from a library
- Revenue model: Freemium app + contractor referral fees
- Competition: Professional energy auditors ($300-500), thermal camera makers' own apps
