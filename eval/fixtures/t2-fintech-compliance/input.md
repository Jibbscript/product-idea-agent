# Test Idea: FinComply Auto

Automated SOC 2 compliance platform for seed-stage fintech startups. Integrates with existing tools (AWS, GitHub, Slack) to automatically collect evidence and generate policies. Reduces compliance timeline from 6+ months to 30 days.

## Additional Context

- Target: Seed to Series A fintech startups (5-30 employees)
- Problem: Enterprise customers require SOC 2, but process is slow and expensive
- Current solutions: $50K+ consultants, 6+ month timeline
- Revenue model: SaaS subscription ($500-1000/month)
- Competitors: Vanta, Drata, Secureframe (but all focused on larger companies)
